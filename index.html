About the measurement

Mount the phone flat and firm against the seat rail, floor or console, not loose on a cushion. Phones sample motion at roughly 60–100 Hz, so anything above half that (shown in the header) cannot be seen: high engine orders at high rpm and noise-type problems are out of range. Felt shake from tyres and propshafts sits well inside it. Use from the passenger seat.
